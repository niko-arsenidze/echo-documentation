Google Platform connects to the external tools your team already relies on so you can keep work in sync without switching tabs. Each integration is authorized on a per-workspace basis and, once configured, is available to every member within that workspace. Admins control which integrations are enabled, and individual members manage their own notification or account-linking preferences within each one.

## Available Integrations

## Setting Up an Integration

Each integration follows the same four-step authorization flow. Follow the steps below for the tool you want to connect.

### Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Slack">
    Locate the Slack card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    You are redirected to Slack's OAuth page. Sign in to your Slack workspace and click **Allow** to grant the required permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Choose which events trigger notifications (e.g., task assignments, status changes) and select the default Slack channel for workspace-wide alerts. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### GitHub

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select GitHub">
    Locate the GitHub card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Sign in to GitHub when prompted and click **Authorize Google Platform** to grant repository read access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Select the repositories you want to link, then map GitHub labels or milestones to Google Platform statuses. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Jira

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Jira">
    Locate the Jira card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Enter your Jira site URL and click **Authorize**. You are redirected to Atlassian's authentication page — sign in and approve the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Map Jira projects to Google Platform projects and define how status fields translate between the two tools. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Google Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Navigate to **Settings → Integrations** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Google Workspace">
    Locate the Google Workspace card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Access">
    Sign in with your Google account and grant Google Platform permission to read your Drive files and profile information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Choose whether to enable single sign-on (SSO) for your entire workspace and decide which Drive folders are available for file attachment. Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing an Integration

To disconnect an integration, navigate to **Settings → Integrations**, find the connected tool, and click the **···** overflow menu on its card. Select **Remove Integration** and confirm the dialog. Google Platform immediately revokes its access token — no data is deleted, but any linked items will lose their live sync connection.

If you want to reconnect the same tool later, repeat the setup steps above from the beginning.

<Tip>
  If an integration card appears grayed out with a lock icon, your workspace Admin has restricted access to that tool. Contact your Admin to request that it be enabled for your workspace.
</Tip>
