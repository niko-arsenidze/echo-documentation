Managing settings in Google Platform gives you precise control over how your workspace looks, how projects are organized, and how each team member experiences the product day-to-day. Whether you are an Admin rolling out company-wide defaults or an individual customizing your personal view, every option is accessible from a single, consistent Settings panel.

## Workspace Settings

Workspace settings apply to every member of your organization and can only be changed by users with the **Admin** or **Owner** role. To reach them, navigate to **Settings → Workspace** from the left sidebar.

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Organization Name** | The display name shown across the workspace and in exported reports. |
| **Logo** | Upload a PNG or SVG (max 2 MB) to replace the default Google Platform wordmark. |
| **Default Language** | Sets the interface language for all new members joining the workspace. |
| **Time Zone** | Controls how dates and times are displayed in project timelines and activity feeds. |

After you update any workspace setting, click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the panel. Changes take effect immediately for all members.

<Note>
  Workspace-level settings require the **Admin** or **Owner** role. If you do not see the Workspace tab inside Settings, contact your workspace Owner to request an elevated role.
</Note>

## Project Settings

Project settings are scoped to a single project and can be updated by any member with at least the **Editor** role on that project. Open a project, then go to **Project Settings** in the top-right action menu.

**Organization Name**
Enter a clear, descriptive name that teammates can identify at a glance. Project names must be unique within a workspace and can be up to 100 characters long.

**Description**
Add a short summary of the project's purpose. This text appears in search results and on the project card in the workspace home view.

**Visibility**
Control who can discover and access the project. The three levels are **Private**, **Internal**, and **Public**. For a full explanation of each level and its security implications, see [Access Permissions and Role Management](/configuration/permissions).

Click **Update Project** to apply your changes.

## Personal Preferences

Personal preferences are stored per-user and never affect other members of the workspace. Access them by clicking your avatar in the bottom-left corner and selecting **Preferences**.

**Theme**
Choose between **Light** and **Dark** mode. Your selection syncs across all browsers where you are signed in.

**Language**
Override the workspace default language for your own account. This changes button labels, tooltips, and system-generated messages.

**Date Format**
Select how dates are displayed throughout the interface:

- `MM/DD/YYYY` — US format
- `DD/MM/YYYY` — International format
- `YYYY-MM-DD` — ISO 8601 format

Preferences save automatically as you change them — no Save button is required.
