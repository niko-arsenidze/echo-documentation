## What You Can Do

Google Platform gives your team the tools to work faster and more cohesively. Here's what you can accomplish:

- **Create and manage projects** — Organize work into projects with dedicated members, timelines, and resources.
- **Collaborate in real time** — Share updates, leave comments, and co-edit documents alongside your teammates.
- **Control access and permissions** — Assign roles at the organization, project, or resource level to keep data secure.
- **Integrate with your existing tools** — Connect Google Platform to third-party services via webhooks and the REST API.
- **Search across everything** — Use global search to instantly find projects, files, teammates, and activity history.
- **Track progress with dashboards** — Monitor project health, team activity, and key metrics from a single view.
