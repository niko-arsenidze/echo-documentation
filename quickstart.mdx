Getting up and running on Google Platform takes only a few minutes. Follow the steps below to sign in, personalize your account, launch your first project, and bring your teammates on board — no technical setup required.

## Set Up in Four Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    Navigate to your organization's Google Platform URL — your admin will share this with you when your account is created. On the login screen, choose **Sign in with SSO** to authenticate through your company's identity provider, or enter your email address and password if your organization uses direct credentials. Once authenticated, you land on your personal dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your Profile">
    Click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Profile Settings**. Update your **display name** so teammates can identify you easily, upload a profile **avatar**, and review your **notification preferences** to control which activity alerts you receive by email or in-app. Save your changes before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Project">
    From the dashboard, click **New Project** in the top navigation bar. Enter a **project name** and an optional **description** that explains the project's purpose to future members. Choose a visibility setting — **Private** (invite-only) or **Organization-wide** (visible to everyone in your organization) — then click **Create Project** to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Teammates">
    Open your new project and navigate to **Project Settings → Members**. In the **Invite Members** field, enter the email addresses of your colleagues (comma-separate multiple addresses). Select a role — **Viewer**, **Editor**, or **Admin** — for each invitee, then click **Send Invites**. Your teammates receive an email with a direct link to join the project.
  </Step>
</Steps>
