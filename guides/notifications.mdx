Google Platform sends you notifications so you never miss an important update — but too many alerts can become a distraction. Use the settings described below to tune exactly which events reach you, how often, and through which channel, so your notification stream stays useful without overwhelming you.

## Notification Types

Google Platform delivers notifications through two channels:

- **Email** — messages sent to your registered work address.
- **In-App** — alerts that appear in the bell icon (🔔) menu at the top of every page.

The table below shows the default notification behavior for each event type:

| Event | Email | In-App |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Mentions (`@you`) | ✅ | ✅ |
| Project updates | ✅ | ✅ |
| New member joined | ❌ | ✅ |
| Task assigned to you | ✅ | ✅ |
| Due date approaching | ✅ | ✅ |

You can override these defaults at both the global level and the individual project level.

## Configuring Per-Project Notifications

Override global defaults for any specific project to raise or lower the signal from that project alone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the project">
    Navigate to the project whose notifications you want to adjust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access notification settings">
    Click the **bell icon** at the top of the project page (next to the project name), then select **Notification Settings** from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your subscription level">
    Select one of the following options:

    - **All Activity** — receive notifications for every event in the project.
    - **Participating Only** — receive notifications only for events where you are mentioned or directly involved (default).
    - **Watching** — receive notifications for high-priority events such as mentions and task assignments only.
    - **Muted** — disable all notifications from this project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your preference">
    Your selection saves automatically. Changes take effect immediately for new events.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting a Digest Schedule

Instead of receiving individual emails for each event, configure a digest to batch notifications into a single daily or weekly summary.

1. Go to **Account Settings** by clicking your avatar in the top-right corner and selecting **Settings**.
2. Open the **Notifications** tab.
3. Under **Email Frequency**, select one of:
   - **Immediate** — each notification triggers a separate email (default).
   - **Daily Digest** — receive one email per day summarizing all activity. Choose the delivery time using the time picker.
   - **Weekly Digest** — receive one email per week. Choose the delivery day and time.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

Digest emails group notifications by project, making it easy to scan activity across all your workspaces in one read.

## Do Not Disturb Mode

Enable Do Not Disturb (DND) to silence all in-app notifications during focus time. Email notifications are unaffected by DND — configure your email digest schedule separately if you want to reduce email volume as well.

### Enable DND Manually

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Set Do Not Disturb** from the dropdown.
3. Choose a duration: **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, **Until tomorrow**, or **Custom**.

DND ends automatically when the chosen duration expires. A crescent moon icon (🌙) appears next to your avatar to remind you that DND is active.

### Schedule DND Automatically

1. Go to **Account Settings → Notifications**.
2. Under **Do Not Disturb Schedule**, toggle **Enable recurring schedule**.
3. Set your start time, end time, and the days of the week when DND should activate automatically.
4. Click **Save Preferences**.

Teammates who mention you while DND is active still see their mention delivered — you simply receive the in-app notification after DND ends rather than in real time.
