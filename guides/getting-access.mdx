Before you can start working in Google Platform, you need a provisioned account. The process is straightforward: request access from the right person, accept your invitation, and complete a short onboarding sequence to get your workspace ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Contact Your Admin">
    Reach out to your IT helpdesk or team admin to request a Google Platform account. Provide your work email address and the team or project you need access to. If you are unsure who to contact, check your company's internal directory or ask your manager to submit the request on your behalf.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the Invitation Email">
    Once your admin provisions your account, you will receive an invitation email from Google Platform. Open the email and click the **Activate your account** link. The link is valid for 72 hours — if it expires, ask your admin to resend the invitation.

    <Note>
      If the invitation email does not appear in your inbox within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder. Ask your IT team to allowlist the sender domain if emails are being filtered consistently.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign In">
    After activating your account, sign in using one of the following methods:

    - **SSO (Single Sign-On):** Click **Continue with SSO** on the login page and authenticate through your company's identity provider (e.g., Okta, Azure AD, or Google Workspace).
    - **Password:** If your organization does not use SSO, set a password when prompted during activation, then sign in with your email and new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Onboarding">
    On your first login, Google Platform walks you through a short onboarding checklist:

    1. **Fill in your profile** — add your display name, job title, and profile photo.
    2. **Review the Terms of Service** — read and accept the platform's usage terms.
    3. **Confirm your notification preferences** — choose how you want to receive alerts (see [Notifications](/guides/notifications) for details).
    4. **Join your first project** — your admin may have pre-assigned you to a project, or you can request access to one.

    Complete all steps to unlock full platform functionality.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Provisioning Timeline

Account provisioning typically follows these timeframes depending on your organization's setup:

| Scenario | Expected Timeline |
|---|---|
| Standard IT helpdesk request | 1–2 business days |
| Manager-approved self-service | Same business day |
| Bulk onboarding (new team) | 3–5 business days |
| SSO configuration required | Up to 1 week (involves IT) |

If your account has not been provisioned after the expected window, follow up with your IT helpdesk and reference your original request ticket. For urgent access needs, ask your manager to escalate the request directly.
