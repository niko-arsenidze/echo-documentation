Before you dive into projects and collaboration, take a few minutes to configure your Google Platform account. A complete profile helps your teammates recognize you, the right notification settings keep your inbox manageable, and enabling security features protects your work and your organization's data.

<Note>
  If your organization enforces Single Sign-On (SSO), some account settings — such as your password and linked email addresses — are managed by your identity provider and may not be editable inside Google Platform. Contact your IT admin if you need to make changes to those fields.
</Note>

## Profile Settings

Your profile is how teammates find and recognize you across Google Platform. To update it, click your avatar in the top-right corner and select **Profile Settings**.

From the **Profile** tab you can:

- **Display name** — Enter the name you want teammates to see on comments, assignments, and activity feeds.
- **Avatar** — Upload a photo or image (PNG or JPG, max 2 MB) to replace the default initials icon.
- **Job title and department** — Add your role to help colleagues understand your context at a glance.
- **Timezone** — Set your local timezone so that due dates and activity timestamps display correctly for you.

Click **Save Changes** after updating any field.

## Notification Preferences

Google Platform sends notifications for project activity, @mentions, task assignments, and system announcements. Tailor these to fit your working style so you stay informed without inbox overload.

Navigate to **Profile Settings → Notifications** to configure:

| Notification Type | Email | In-App |
|---|---|---|
| @mentions and direct replies | On by default | On by default |
| Task assigned to you | On by default | On by default |
| Project status updates | Off by default | On by default |
| Weekly digest | On by default | — |
| System announcements | On by default | On by default |

Toggle each channel independently, then click **Save Preferences**.

## Security — Two-Factor Authentication

Enabling two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second layer of protection to your account. Once enabled, you must supply a one-time code from your authenticator app each time you sign in with a password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Security Settings">
    Click your avatar and go to **Profile Settings → Security**. Under the **Two-Factor Authentication** section, click **Enable 2FA**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Authentication Method">
    Select **Authenticator App** (recommended — works with Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, and others) or **SMS** if your admin has enabled text-message delivery for your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR Code">
    Open your authenticator app and scan the QR code displayed on screen. If you can't scan it, click **Enter code manually** to copy the setup key into your app instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and Save">
    Enter the six-digit code generated by your authenticator app into the **Verification Code** field, then click **Confirm**. Google Platform displays a set of single-use **backup codes** — store these somewhere safe (such as a password manager) in case you lose access to your authentication device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once 2FA is active, a green **Enabled** badge appears next to the Two-Factor Authentication setting. To disable or reset 2FA, click **Manage** in the same section and follow the prompts.

## Linked Accounts

Linking additional email addresses to your account lets you receive project invitations sent to any of your work addresses and consolidates notifications in one place.

To manage linked accounts, go to **Profile Settings → Linked Accounts**:

1. Click **Add Email Address** and enter the address you want to link.
2. Check that inbox for a verification email from Google Platform and click the confirmation link.
3. Once verified, the address appears in your linked accounts list with a **Verified** badge.

To remove a linked address, click the **···** menu next to it and select **Remove**. Your primary email address (used to sign in) cannot be removed without first designating a new primary.
